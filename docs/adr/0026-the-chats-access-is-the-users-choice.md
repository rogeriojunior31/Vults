# 0026. Who answers the chat's cards is the user's choice

**Status:** Merged into [0004](0004-a-human-answers-permissions.md) on 2026-10-12.

In Zeca's chat, the user chooses in Settings who answers its cards: a card each time, their
policies, or full access, with destructive calls, a policy's deny or ask, and content from outside
still bringing cards.
