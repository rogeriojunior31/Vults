# 0020. 0.2.0 at the Operations gate; the companion, the phone and the rest in 0.2.x

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-10. Supersedes [0015](0015-everything-by-0.2-in-small-releases.md).

## Context

[0015](0015-everything-by-0.2-in-small-releases.md) put everything planned before 0.2.0, the
Operations milestone, in small 0.1.x releases. The plan that replaced `road-to-0.2.md`'s open steps
(`docs/dev/plan-zeca.md`) grew: Zeca's memory and work first, then a companion that speaks when it
should, an Android phone, and leftovers such as the side panel and the desktop bird. Holding 0.2.0
until all of it is done would keep the Operations milestone waiting months for work it does not
need.

## Decision

- Releases stay small and frequent: a **0.1.x** goes out whenever one or two steps are merged and the
  R3 smoke check passes; urgent fixes are the next one too. Patch numbers may pass 9.
- **0.2.0** is tagged at the gate of `plan-zeca.md`'s wave 4: the Nest, policies
  ([0014](0014-consent-before-autonomy.md)), tasks, starting agents from the app and their review.
- After it, waves 5 to 7 (the companion, the phone, the leftovers) ship as **0.2.x** releases under
  the same rule.
- Linux first through 0.2.x, with Android as the one exception
  ([0017](0017-a-signed-phone-decision-is-a-click.md)). Windows and macOS come after.
- 1.0 still means "done for Linux".

## Consequences

- Steps that 0015 promised by 0.2.0 and the plan now puts later (the command palette, open-mic
  voice, the side panel, the desktop bird, Vercel, the voice dictionary) arrive in 0.2.x.
- `road-to-0.2.md`, *Releases*, and `plan-zeca.md`, *Waves*, point at this record.
- A patch number still does not mean "fixes only": 0.1.x and 0.2.x releases carry features.
