# The Nest

The Nest is Vults' own window, for when the island is too small: the whole flock, your tasks, the
chat, what happened and your connections. Open it from the tray icon (**Nest…**) or with the arrow
at the top of the island's chat. It is built when you open it and gone when you close it, and it
opens with Zeca off too, without its Chat.

Five tabs sit at the top, then the size switch and the gear, which opens Settings. The switch keeps
the tab you are on: **Room** is the window, **Full** the whole screen (**Esc** goes back to Room),
**Quick** closes the Nest and opens the island, **Mini** closes it and leaves the pill. **Alt+1** to
**Alt+5** pick a tab. At the bottom, **Zeca on** / **Zeca off**
switches him (as **Settings → Flock → Zeca** does) and **Back to the island** brings the island up.

## Flock

The flock at twice its size in its habitat, then every session in a row: its project, its
repository and branch, its agent and what it is doing. A click on a bird or a row (or **↑ ↓**) puts
that session in front, and its details show: what it changed, its last steps, its folder,
repository, branch, editor and cost, and with Zeca on **Ask Zeca about this session**, which opens
the Chat with the question ready. **Needs you** keeps only the sessions waiting for you; **Find a
session…** reads projects, repositories, branches, agents and what each is doing now. In a wide window or Full the
details sit beside the rows.

A session that waits for you shows **Review on the island**: only the island answers a card
([Approving from the island](approvals.md)), and the button brings it up with that session in
front. **Open terminal** shows where Vults can bring its terminal forward
([The island](island.md#getting-to-a-session)).

## Tasks

Your [tasks](chat.md#tasks), under the roost each belongs to (the ones with none last): each with
its goal, criteria, owner, status and cost, why the router chose its agent, its review rounds as
marks (up to three, the last in its verdict's color), the buttons that start, review or hand it
off, and today's spend against your daily budget. **New task** keeps one without the chat, which
is how you make one with Zeca off.

## Chat

The island's conversation with Zeca, wide, with replies in full Markdown (a link shows its address
and is never followed); a message, **New** or another engine in either one shows in both. Cards
show here too, but only the island answers them: **Answer on the island** brings its chat up.
In a wide window or Full, **Recent** sits beside it: your saved conversations, the one going on
marked, and **New conversation**; a click goes on with one. **Memory** opens what Zeca remembers
beside the conversation: what waits for your yes, the files and
their history ([Zeca's memory](memory.md)). Without Zeca there is no Chat tab.

## Activity

What this computer keeps, read only here:

- **Week**: the week's recap and the year's grid, with the history's switches ([Activity](activity.md)).
- **By project**: a card for each repository a session works in: its folders and branches, its
  sessions, your pull requests and checks (from the GitHub card), what Zeca remembers of the
  project and what its tasks cost ([Zeca's memory](memory.md#project-memory)).
- **History**: each finished turn of your agents, counts only.
- **Cost**: your plans' usage windows and what the agents cost over the last two weeks, against
  your daily budget, and by roost ([Cost and a daily budget](chat.md#cost-and-a-daily-budget)).
- **Traces**: Zeca's own work, each chat turn with its model, tokens and cost, and under it each
  tool card with how long it waited for an answer; kept 30 days.
- **Audit**: who answered each card and how (you, your phone, an Always rule or a policy you
  accepted); kept 90 days and never changed.

## Connections

The card of each connector you turned on, as the island shows it: for GitHub, your pull requests,
the reviews waiting for you and your default branches, each with its checks. A row opens on GitHub.
**Manage connections** opens **Settings → Connectors** ([Connectors](connectors.md)).
