Write a context pack of this task for whoever takes it over, who will know nothing else. Use exactly these headings, in this order, each part short, as bullet points (none for a part with nothing):

## Objective
## Constraints
## Done
## In progress
## Decisions
## Files
## Failures
## Next

Objective: the goal in one or two sentences. Constraints: what must hold or must not be done. Done and In progress: what is finished and what is half done, by the criteria. Decisions: what was chosen and why. Files: the files that matter, with a few words each. Failures: what was tried and did not work, so it is not tried again. Next: the next concrete steps. Write nothing before the first heading or after the last part.
